This week, I finished implementing and testing the main features of my Slingshot Destruction simulation. I was successfully able to improve the collision solver to reduce instability when blocks were resting on one another. This change accounted for a energy drift reduction from 10.4% to 0.64% in my standard tests. I also added more physics diagnostics, performance monitoring, and a limit on the number of active particles to help prevent performance issues and potential lagging. All seven of my numerical validation checks passed with flying colors, and the simulation has been tested under CPU throttling metrics.

I do still wish to improve the overall gameplay experience, especially how the structures respond to repeated impacts and potentially adding generative features to create more interesting structures. I feel the project itself is ready for the final submission in terms of what it is capable of doing. I do however wish to use my remaining time to polish the overall experience and gamification thereof.
