I chose this project because I wanted to create something interactive and quite honestly something that breaks away from the traditional physics world we are used to. Furthermore, I wanted it to be something that is entertaining while still incorporating as many physics concepts as possible. An Angry Birds-style game seemed like a good way to combine projectile motion, collisions, momentum, energy, and rotational dynamics into one simulation. Most of all, it will allow me to very easily build onto the physics engine we have already developed throughout the semester.

I will say however, that my biggest concern is getting the collisions between multiple blocks to behave realistically. Individual collisions have worked well in testing, but when several blocks interact, maintaining stability becomes more difficult. I have already encountered issues with excessive bouncing, energy changes, and blocks behaving unpredictably. I believe the hardest part will be balancing realistic physics with a simulation that remains stable and remains as something that is enjoyable to use.
