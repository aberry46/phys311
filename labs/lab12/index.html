Slingshot Destruction

PHYS 311 Final Project | Interactive Rigid-Body Physics

Launch projectiles into wooden block structures to destroy targets. Explore projectile motion, rotational dynamics, collision impulses, and momentum transfer in an interactive two-dimensional physics simulation.

How to Play

Click or touch near the slingshot launcher. Drag backward to adjust launch speed and direction, then release to fire. Use projectile impacts to topple wooden blocks and destroy targets. Reset the scene to attempt another shot.

Keyboard Shortcuts: Space = Pause/Resume | . = Single Step | R = Reset | + / - = Speed
