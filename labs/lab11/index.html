PHYS 311: Interactive Multi-Body Simulation

This interactive physics sandbox demonstrates how forces, collisions, and numerical integration affect the motion of objects. Explore four different physical systems, adjust parameters, and observe changes in energy and momentum conservation. In the Billiards scene, drag existing bodies to apply forces or launch new bodies using the slingshot. Use the visualization controls to examine motion trails, velocity vectors, and energy over time.

Keyboard Shortcuts: Space = Pause/Resume | . = Single Step | R = Reset | + / - = Speed

Mouse / Touch Controls: In the Billiards scene, select and drag an existing body to apply a spring force. Click an empty area and drag backward to aim a new projectile. Release to launch.
